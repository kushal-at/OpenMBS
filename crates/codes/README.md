# openmbs-codes

Building codes, environmental hazard generation, and steel member capacity design checks.

## Supported Specifications
- **ASCE/SEI 7-22**:
  - Directional MWFRS wind pressure distribution ($K_z$, $q_z$, $G$, $C_p$, $\pm GC_{pi}$)
  - Flat and sloped roof snow loads ($p_f$, $p_s$)
  - Roof live load reduction ($R_1$, $R_2$)
  - Equivalent lateral seismic force ($C_s$)
  - Full LRFD, ASD, and unfactored serviceability drift/deflection load combinations.
- **AISC 360-16**:
  - Chapter B compactness classification (compact, noncompact, slender)
  - Chapter E compression buckling ($F_{cr}$)
  - Chapter F flexural buckling (LTB, FLB) with slender-web reduction factor ($R_{pg}$)
  - Chapter G web shear coefficient ($C_{v1}$)
  - Chapter H combined axial-flexural interaction equations ($H1$-1a / $H1$-1b).
- **AISI S100-16**: Cold-formed secondary member checks (moment capacity, effective width, and deflections).
- **PE Certification Letter**: Automated plain-text and Markdown PE Stamped Design Certification Letter generation.
