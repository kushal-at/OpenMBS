# openmbs-drawings

Professional drafting, CNC fabrication profiles, and BIM export engine.

## Output Deliverables
- **Sheet E1 (SVG)**: Scaled Anchor Bolt & Foundation Reaction Plan.
- **Sheet RF1 (SVG)**: Rigid Frame Shop Assembly drawing with member piece marks and connection callouts.
- **Shop DXF (`shop_RF1.dxf`)**: 1:1 full-scale CNC profile and bolt-hole coordinates in millimetres.
- **IFC4 STEP BIM (`model.ifc`)**: ISO-10303-21 STEP structural model compatible with Revit, Tekla Structures, and Trimble Connect.
