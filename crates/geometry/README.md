# openmbs-geometry

Parametric 3D Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) geometry, openings, and spatial modeling.

## Capabilities
- **Building Geometry (`BuildingSpec`)**: Unequal bay spacings, asymmetric roof ridges, independent sidewall eave heights, and braced bays.
- **Attachments & Subsystems**: Lean-tos, sidewall/endwall canopies, parapet walls, overhead bridge crane runways, and portal frames.
- **4-Wall Openings (`WallOpening`)**: Framed openings with shop-framed vs field-located configurations.
- **Girt Trimming (`trimmed_girt_segments`)**: Exact geometric trimming and segmentation of horizontal girts around doors and windows.
- **Spatial Bounding Volumes (`Aabb3`)**: 3D Axis-Aligned Bounding Box calculation for structural members and clash detection.
