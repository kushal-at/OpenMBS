Built 1:1 to Match the MBS Workflow

Engineers familiar with Metal Building Software will feel right at home with the exact design tabs.

Shape (UI-0002: Geometry & Elevations)

Define parametric building width, eave heights, roof slopes, and base elevations.

  • Symmetrical gable, asymmetrical gable, and single-slope configurations
  • Peak offset / Ridge X position with automatic pitch calculation (e.g. 1:10 / 4:12)
  • Fixed column bases vs pinned base supports
  • Post & Beam vs Expandable Rigid Frame endwall framing

Bay Spacing (UI-0003 & UI-0004)

Flexible bay spacing along the length with real-time remaining distance feedback.

  • Unequal bay lengths (e.g. 20ft, 25ft, 25ft, 20ft) with exact tributary width tracking
  • Designated X-braced bays for roof and sidewall rod bracing
  • Roof purlin spacing o.c. and nesting lap length allowance
  • Sidewall & endwall girt spacings with flange brace unbraced length Lb

Code & Loads (UI-0006)

Automated ASCE 7-22 environmental hazard generator and load combinations.

  • ASCE 7-22 Directional MWFRS wind pressure distribution (Kz, qz, G, Cp, ±GCpi)
  • Flat and sloped roof snow loads (pf, ps) with slippery roof reduction
  • Roof live load reduction (R1, R2) and superimposed collateral dead loads
  • Full LRFD and ASD combinations with iterative 2nd-order P-Delta analysis

Rigid Frames (UI-0005)

Linearly tapered 3-plate built-up I-sections solved via 5-point Gauss quadrature.

  • AISC 360-16 Chapter F5 slender-web bending reduction factor (Rpg)
  • Lateral-torsional buckling (LTB) and compression flange local buckling (FLB)
  • AISC Design Guide 4 Bolted Moment End-Plate connections (eave knee & ridge)
  • AISC Design Guide 1 column base plates with DM81 anchor bolt supply scope

  • Live overlap and wall boundary validation checks
